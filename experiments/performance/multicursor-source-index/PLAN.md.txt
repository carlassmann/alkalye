# Preserve exact multicursor edits in the source index

Source-only candidate: `SourceTextarea.replace` already receives exact original-coordinate edits from `multiInput`, but `apply` currently collapses them to one broad difference and rebuilds that entire text-index span. Candidate certifies the edits against both source strings, then applies them backwards to preserve unchanged tree nodes. Invalid, overlapping, stale or untrusted ranges keep the original broad fallback.

Do not adopt on source inspection. First compare the real TextIndex public interface for sparse 64, 512 and 2048 edits across a rich one-MiB fixture. Exact final text, line/native/Unicode addressing and fork independence must hold. Descending persistent edits may allocate more branches than a bulk rebuild and lose at high counts.

A favorable module result only admits the candidate to a background browser journey. Verify source/native caret and every selection, distinct accepted revision and receipt, peer edits, Undo and reload. Capture whole interaction tasks and observed exact-state frame opportunities, with actual source/asset hashes. No narrow module timing proves 120Hz app behavior.

Production root is untouched. Candidate is untyped, untested and unmeasured; no gain claimed.
