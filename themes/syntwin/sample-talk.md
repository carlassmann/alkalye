# Preparing a useful conversation

---

# Start with a purpose

<!-- prettier-ignore-start -->
  Who is this twin for?

  What conversation should it make easier?

  What should it say when it does not know?
<!-- prettier-ignore-end -->

Invite the room to name one real audience before discussing features.

---

# Curate the perspective

<!-- prettier-ignore-start -->
  Add the notes, documents, and references a careful person would use.

  Prefer a small, current collection to a large unreviewed archive.
<!-- prettier-ignore-end -->

Pause here: ask what source a new visitor would need first.

---

# Test the conversation

<!-- prettier-ignore-start -->
  Ask an ordinary question.

  Ask a difficult question.

  Notice unsupported certainty, missing context, and an unfamiliar voice.
<!-- prettier-ignore-end -->

---

# Share with a small group

<!-- prettier-ignore-start -->
  Let people try the twin in the context where it will be useful.

  Collect the questions it helped with and the gaps it revealed.

  Review the knowledge before inviting a wider audience.
<!-- prettier-ignore-end -->

---

# Keep the next step small

<!-- prettier-ignore-start -->
  Choose one improvement.

  Make the source clearer.

  Return with what the conversation taught you.
<!-- prettier-ignore-end -->

Close by naming who will review the next version.
